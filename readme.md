# 💰 Expense Tracker

## 📌 Project Overview

**Expense Tracker** is a web application that helps users track and manage their daily income and expenses. It allows users to record transactions, organize them into categories, monitor their monthly spending, and understand their spending habits.

The application helps users identify unnecessary expenses and make better financial decisions through spending analysis and budget tracking.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many households exceed their monthly budgets because of unmonitored discretionary spending on areas such as entertainment, dining, and subscriptions.

Without proper expense tracking, it becomes difficult to understand where money is being spent and identify unnecessary expenses.

**Expense Tracker** solves this problem by allowing users to record transactions, categorize expenses, analyze spending patterns, and manage their monthly budgets effectively.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Track daily income and expenses.
* Categorize transactions for better organization.
* Monitor monthly spending.
